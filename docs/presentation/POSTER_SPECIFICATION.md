# SentinelCrypt AI — Final Presentation & Poster Specification

## 10-Slide Presentation
1. Title and motivation
2. Problem
3. Research question
4. Architecture
5. Dataset/model methodology
6. XAI methodology
7. Cryptographic ledger
8. Results
9. Limitations/future work
10. Demo/GitHub/contact

## Poster
Title; problem; research question; architecture; methodology; key results; limitations; repository/demo QR; contact.

## Design Rule
Prefer diagrams and concise evidence. Do not fill the poster with unexplained jargon.
