# SentinelCrypt AI — Research Context

## Research Hypothesis
By uniting local SHAP explanations with forward-linked cryptographic hash chains, we can achieve high-fidelity intrusion detection whose inference integrity and explanatory accountability can be independently verified without compromising real-time throughput.

## Research Experiments
1. **EXP-A: Cross-Dataset Generalization**
   - **Hypothesis:** Models trained on UNSW-NB15 suffer measurable performance degradation when evaluated on out-of-distribution traffic (CICIDS2017).
   - **Metrics:** F1 drop ($\Delta F_1$), ROC-AUC drop.

2. **EXP-B: Explanation Stability & Fidelity**
   - **Hypothesis:** Local feature attributions generated by TreeSHAP remain stable under Gaussian noise perturbations ($\epsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$) up to a critical noise threshold.
   - **Metrics:** Lipschitz continuity constant, top-5 feature Jaccard similarity.

3. **EXP-C: Ledger Integrity & Tamper Resistance**
   - **Hypothesis:** The SHA-256 forward-linked cryptographic hash chain detects 100% of single-byte and multi-byte historical record modifications with zero false positives.
   - **Metrics:** Tamper detection rate (100%), false positive rate (0%), verification runtime scaling ($O(N)$).

4. **EXP-D: Model Complexity vs. Verification Trade-Offs**
   - **Hypothesis:** Random Forest provides superior detection power compared to Logistic Regression at the expense of higher SHAP computation latency, while cryptographic hashing latency remains constant $O(1)$.
   - **Metrics:** Accuracy vs. SHAP computation time (ms) vs. Hash latency (ms).
