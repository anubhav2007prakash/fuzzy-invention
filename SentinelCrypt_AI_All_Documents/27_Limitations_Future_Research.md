# SentinelCrypt AI — Limitations & Future Research

## Limitations
1. Benchmark datasets may not represent modern operational traffic.
2. Cross-dataset results can be invalid if feature semantics differ.
3. Explanation stability is not explanation correctness.
4. Hash chaining detects certain changes but does not prevent compromise.
5. Local authentication/availability controls are not production-grade.
6. Small experiments may have limited statistical power.
7. Confidence values may require calibration.

## Future Research
Temporal validation; calibration; multiple XAI methods; human analyst evaluation; signed evidence; external trust anchors; larger datasets; repeated experiments with uncertainty estimates.
